How close this is: the price windows are computed from the price curve the way tibber_prices builds its ⌀ Price Next Nh sensors (from the next interval on). The day's forecast high and low are taken from the measured outdoor curve, and so is the hourly forecast behind the demand factor. Windows, presence, weekdays and manual overrides are not simulated. The room is a single-node model; it is good for comparing two parameter sets on the same day, not for predicting your bill. Calibrate it by loading a measured room temperature and adjusting Room model until the curves match.

How close this is: the model was fitted to what your thermometers did when the units, the heater, the windows, the sun and the weather did what they did, and it was checked on days it had not seen. It knows nothing about people, cooking or doors you opened that have no sensor, so a single day can be off by a few tenths of a degree. Use it to compare settings on the same day; the absolute kWh lean on the heat pump's efficiency, which the phase reading can only roughly pin down.
